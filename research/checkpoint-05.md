# Operation Sindoor: investigative checkpoint 05

**Evidence cutoff:** 19 October 2026  
**Status:** Independent, incomplete, open-source fact-finding. **Supersedes only the specified qualifications of checkpoint 04**, not its other findings.  
**Scope:** Pahalgam attacker-count provenance, authenticated-vs-unauthenticated suspect reports, separate NIA cases, UN Security Council correspondence and precise effect of the 31 August 2026 Indus Waters Treaty orders.

## Findings and evidence hierarchy

### 1. UN five attackers versus NIA three: the *underlying information cutoff* matters

[UN Security Council document S/2025/482](https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2127961/n2517600.pdf), issued **24 July 2025**, prints the **30 June 2025** Monitoring Team cover letter, which expressly says information was considered **up to 22 June 2025** (page 2). Its paragraph 84 (page 16) says five attackers killed 26 Pahalgam civilians; TRF claimed responsibility on 22 April and repeated it on 23 April, then retracted on 26 April. It also differentiates incompatible member-state views on TRF's relationship with Lashkar-e-Taiba.

The [NIA's *23 June 2025* clarification](https://nia.gov.in/update-investigations-pehalgam-case-and-clarification-regarding-speculationsonpahalgamterror-attack) (the linked official PDF is also indexed at [the NIA secure-PDF endpoint](https://nia.gov.in/secure-pdf/view/16d734c7c2a86d857989aadd0f2d3cba)) expressly says the interrogation of two arrested men disclosed the identities of **three armed terrorists**, and lists eyewitness accounts, videos, technical information and police sketches. Critically, the NIA also said it had **not yet reached conclusions about the identities** at that time; it warned about speculative press drawings. [BBC contemporary reporting](https://feeds.bbci.co.uk/news/articles/c36xe95g06zo) contemporaneously reflects the three-gunman NIA narrative.

The [NIA's 15 December 2025 original press-release PDF](https://nia.gov.in/sites/default/files/Document/enc_af29c437cd8b7bce6e3451e577a3f824.pdf) identifies the three alleged gunmen later killed in Operation Mahadev and states there are six individual accused plus LeT/TRF as a charged legal entity. It is a prosecution announcement, **not** a published judge's evaluation of ballistic, DNA or communications exhibits. The 6 July 2026 supplementary chargesheet is retained in checkpoint 04.

**Correction to prior temporal shorthand:** The UN report was *published* after the NIA's three-gunman statements, but its information cutoff came **one day before** NIA's 23 June public clarification. Therefore this is an unresolved difference between two dated information streams, not proof the UN rejected the later NIA assessment. The precise UN five-person sourcing and possible role differences remain unreconciled. The report's anonymous Member State assessments cannot be converted into a unanimous finding that Pakistan's government directed the attack.

### 2. Defence Ministry disavowed an online alleged suspect-background report

[India's Ministry of Defence press note, 4 August 2025, Release 2152335](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2152335&lang=2&reg=3) expressly says a circulating report on the identities/backgrounds of the Pahalgam attackers was **not prepared or issued by any authorized Armed Forces media channel** and that no corresponding comments were made by nominated military spokespersons.

**Assessment:** The authenticity of an alleged 'Services report' circulated online cannot be assumed. The MOD's disavowal does **not** disprove the NIA's own chargesheet, which is a distinct agency's later official legal allegation. Secure the original circulating document, provenance and the named issuer before citing anything within it as a Defence Ministry finding.

### 3. 12 October 2026 LeT property attachment is **a different case**, not new Pahalgam proof

The [NIA release dated 12 October 2026](https://nia.gov.in/nia-attaches-property-used-harbour-pakistani-let-terrorists-srinagar) announces attachment of a Srinagar residential property in connection with an alleged LeT support network. A [contemporaneous wire-style report reproducing the announcement](https://www.kashmirnewsobserver.com/top-stories/nia-attaches-srinagar-property-used-to-harbour-pak-let-terrorists-kno-206484) and [The New Indian Express reporting](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Oct/12/nia-attaches-srinagar-house-for-allegedly-harbouring-let-militants) identify **RC-02/2026/NIA/JMU**, arising from FIR 24/2026. The Pahalgam prosecution is **RC-02/2025/NIA/JMU**.

**Finding:** Identical or similar organization names, regional locations or consecutive case-series numbers are insufficient to merge the two files. No original record examined here establishes the 2026 property as an April 2025 Pahalgam attack location. Record separately, but **do not add** a Pahalgam accused, facility or confirmed link from this October announcement. An attachment is an investigative/legal action, not proof of guilt.

### 4. Treaty award: narrow jurisdictional holdings and exact temporal wording

The 31 August 2026 proceeding is the [Indus Waters Western Rivers Arbitration, PCA Case 2023-01](https://pca-cpa.org/en/cases/284/), with the PCA acting as registry. The [publicly reproduced award text](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-pakistan-v-india-award-on-the-status-of-the-indus-waters-treaty-in-respect-of-india-s-decision-on-abeyance-monday-31st-august-2026) supplies several important distinctions:

- **Paragraph 262:** The Court says it **does not need to determine** whether the alleged terrorist attacks occurred or are attributable to Pakistan to answer the treaty-suspension question. The award is *not* a judicial exoneration or conviction concerning Pahalgam.
- **Paragraph 290:** The Court expressly acknowledges a **four-day period of aerial combat and missile strikes in May 2025**, ending on 10 May, but concludes the states **were not at that time (August 2026) engaged in an international armed conflict**. Thus a headline such as 'tribunal said India and Pakistan never fought in 2025' would be false.
- **Operative paragraphs 326–327:** The Court treats India's April 2025 'abeyance' announcement as an asserted suspension/termination, holds it unsupported by the treaty or invoked wider international-law grounds, declares the treaty still in force, reserves costs and other issues for later decisions, and remains seized of the dispute.

The accompanying [31 August 2026 interim-measures order, publicly reproduced](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-pakistan-v-india-order-on-the-interim-measures-application-of-pakistan-dated-4-march-2026-monday-31st-august-2026) concerns **specific Ratle hydroelectric project work**, not a blanket ban on all construction. Paragraph 286 identifies specified categories of concreting/intake-structure work and notification of schedule changes. Paragraph 287 explicitly states that other work is **not prohibited** except as specified in 286(A) and 286(B). Some restrictions extend until a neutral expert's final decision; one identified intake-structure restriction extends until 90 days after that decision. These are operative directions of a tribunal **whose competence India expressly rejects**.

Read [India's 31 August 2026 official response](https://eoibelgrade.gov.in/index.php/listview/NzE2) alongside the [PCA award notice](https://pca-cpa.org/en/news/pca-press-release-pca-case-no-2023-01-the-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-islamic-republic-of-pakistan-v-republic-of-india-6/). India's rejection persists; the award alone is not a direct measurement of dam operations, actual water releases, construction compliance or political resolution.

**Document-access qualification:** PCA metadata and independently indexed reproduced *operative award/order wording* were examined. The complete signed 6.71 MB PCA award PDF and 3.03 MB order PDF were not independently downloaded, paginated, or cryptographically hashed in this pass. Cite the reproduced text as a reproduction, not falsely as a personally inspected original PDF.

### 5. Newly mapped UN correspondence, without overstating its legal effect

The United Nations Digital Library individually indexes three **Pakistani Security Council letters** directly linked to the May 2025 confrontation:

- [S/2025/289](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4081956?ln=en), letter *dated 6 May 2025*, concerning the alleged May 6–7 Indian strikes (UN record distributed 7 May).
- [S/2025/290](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082002?ln=en), letter *dated 7 May 2025*, transmitting a press release relating to the strikes.
- [S/2025/299](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082024?ln=en), letter *dated 8 May 2025*, transmitting a Ministry of Foreign Affairs response to Indian media assertions.

Their **existence, dates and formal UN record identifiers** are established from UN catalogue metadata. The PDFs could not be opened in this pass. **Do not label any of these an Article 51 notification** until the actual annex and letter language has been examined. Pakistan's [7 May NSC statement](https://mofa.gov.pk/press-releases/the-national-security-committee-nsc-was-convened-under-the-chairmanship-of-the-prime-minister-2?mission=abidjan) separately invoked Article 51; that public invocation is not itself proof of submission of a specific Article 51 letter. India's [7 May original Foreign Secretary statement](https://www.indembassybern.gov.in/section/press-releases/statement-by-foreign-secretary-operation-sindoor-dated-07-05-2025/) explicitly asserted impending future attacks but did not publish the underlying intelligence.
