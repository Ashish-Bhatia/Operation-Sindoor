# Operation Sindoor: investigative checkpoint 08

**Evidence cutoff:** 19 October 2026  
**Continuation:** [Checkpoint 07](checkpoint-07.md) and all earlier records remain preserved. This pass makes **dated casualty revisions**, expands **civilian aircraft-crash harm**, and reconciles **Poonch housing payments and local injury figures**.  
**Status:** Incomplete, source-attributed independent public-record investigation, not a court decision, aircraft-serial audit, or complete bilateral fatality roster.

## Executive findings

**A. Pakistan's publicly reported military death toll increased from 11 (13 May 2025) to 13 (14 May 2025).** Original Pakistan state-broadcaster and official-agency accounts both reproduce the 14 May ISPR statement: two hospitalised personnel, **Havildar Muhammad Naveed of the Army** and **Senior Technician Muhammad Ayaz of the Air Force**, died of injuries, producing a revised cumulative count of **13**. This accounts for the previous 5-versus-6 reported PAF fatality discrepancy. It does *not* independently audit every military death, nor establish fighter aircraft lost. Earlier dossier and structured-casualty statements of 11 should be labelled **13 May snapshots, superseded for dated cumulative comparison**.

**B. A Bathinda aircraft crash on 7 May 2025 resulted in at least two reported civilian deaths, rather than only the one recorded contemporaneously.** The initial 7–8 May reports identify farm worker **Govind Kumar** as dead, with nine people injured near Akalia/Aklian Kalan village. A July 2025 and then May 2026 Indian Express follow-up identify **Raj Kumar Singh**, injured in a later explosion after the aircraft crashed, as dying around **11 May**, four days later. The 2026 journalist inspected a **11 May police General Diary detail**, but this project's researchers have not obtained the original signed police document. The **aircraft type**, unique hull, kill mechanism, pilot status, and whether the injury count was updated afterward are **unresolved**. Do **not** merge these two civilians automatically into the separate May 13 minimum 16 civilians reported killed in **cross-border shelling**. Need person-level collision and coverage audit.

**C. The May 2026 Poonch anniversary investigation quotes district-government figures of 14 killed, 65 injured and 598 houses damaged, and reports actual reconstruction disbursement amounts, not merely earmarked central funds.** Indian Express cites disbursement of **₹7.39 crore for pucca homes** and **₹2.25 crore for kuccha homes**. Full signed payment ledgers remain uninspected. It says **200–300 prefabricated homes were proposed**, and an earlier proposal for **5,693 additional bunkers** awaited approval, not that either infrastructure programme had been completed. Figures are a Poonch district/time-specific journalist-attributed government account, **not a new nationwide total**. Compare with the June 2025 MHA additional **₹25 crore allocation for 2,060 houses** across relevant Jammu & Kashmir border districts. Both statements can be true; without account codes, categories and unique property identifiers, **do not assert that the Poonch payments represent the disbursement of the same MHA ₹25 crore**.

**D. Sindh provincial government publicly named the six PAF personnel it said died in the May attacks**, providing direct Pakistan-side geographic damage/casualty evidence at Bholari, but no photographic proof of destroyed aircraft. The additional PAF name, **Muhammad Ayaz**, is consistent with the later ISPR update. Sindh Chief Minister also reported one Ghotki civilian death and injuries at Bholari and Shahbaz bases. These statements increase confidence that personnel losses occurred at specific bases, **not** that any named jet was destroyed there.

**Exactly what changed:** The 14 May ISPR update named **Havildar Muhammad Naveed** and **Senior Technician Muhammad Ayaz** as persons who succumbed to earlier wounds. This is not evidence of two fresh May 14 attacks. The original 11, the two fatalities, and the later total 13 all describe the *same cumulative 2025 confrontation casualty stream*.

**Original Pakistan civilian claim unchanged by this discovery:** **40 civilians killed and 121 injured** as originally reported 13 May, repeated in 16 May UN correspondence, remain attributed official figures not independently person-matched. If another later revision emerges, preserve as a new dated version, never silently overwrite the historic source.

**Sindh source nuance:** [16 May chief-minister statement](https://cm.sindh.gov.pk/news/murad-announces-compensation-for-martyrs-killed-and-injured-during-the-india-attack) listed six PAF dead around the **Bholari** base attack: Squadron Leader Usman Yusuf, Chief Technician Aurangzeb, Senior Technician Najeeb, Technician Farooq, Senior Technician Mubashir, and Senior Technician Muhammad Ayaz. It separately named Ghotki civilian **Mukhtiar Leghari** and described **five personnel wounded at Bholari** and **nine at Shahbaz/Jacobabad**. [Dawn's near-contemporary reporting](https://www.dawn.com/news/1911205/cm-sindh-announces-compensation-for-martyrs-injured-in-indian-attacks) reproduces these names. This demonstrates **official provincial acknowledgement of personnel losses at military bases**. The original provincial webpage was located and indexed but intermittently timed out when opened, so detailed wording was corroborated through Dawn rather than claiming an independently downloaded signed personnel roster. **A personnel death at a base is not proof of aircraft destruction or a pilot's death in flight.**

**Pakistan-side compensation announcement:** Sindh said PKR **10 million** for each person killed and PKR **1 million** per injured person. This is an *announced* entitlement, not a verified bank disbursement; do not compare currencies or benefits with Indian awards without exchange-date and eligibility adjustments.

| [8 May Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/chandigarh-news/one-killed-9-injured-as-unidentified-aircraft-crashes-in-bathinda-village-101746639649233.html) | Government hospital medical officer quoted on initial civilian injuries | Independent newspaper, but substantially same evolving incident |

| [8 May 2026 Indian Express field/police investigation](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/chandigarh/he-died-saving-others-op-sindoor-aircraft-crash-victims-family-in-bathinda-awaits-job-one-year-after-death-10679806/) | Journalist refers to 11 May 2025 General Diary detail and BNSS s.194 process; family reports **₹2 lakh** received and requested job unresolved | Reporter inspected police record; original certified report, hospital death certificate and payment record not acquired |

**Mechanism caution:** Reports describe the aircraft crashing, burning, followed by an explosion when nearby civilians approached and attempted to assist. The police record as described uses an accidental-explosion investigation classification and cites no foul play. That does **not** establish what caused the aircraft to crash in the first place. It is unsound to identify the aircraft as a **Rafale, Mirage 2000, Su-30MKI** or adversary shootdown based only on these civilian testimony reports.

**Casualty accounting:** Two separate crash-related civilian fatalities are supported. The original nine injured reflects the *initial* immediate account; some may subsequently have died, and the injured figure may include Raj Kumar. Do not treat later fatality as an independent tenth injured person. Do not sum Bathinda crash civilians with 16 Indian shelling deaths until the broader source inclusion/exclusion criteria and victim roster are verified. Further evidence needs the police GD number/full page, hospital logs, final police outcome, Air Force accident inquiry and site-based wreckage attribution.

## 3. Poonch district's civilian toll and reconstruction finance, a year later

[Indian Express, 7 May 2026, on-the-ground anniversary report](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/operation-sindoor-a-year-later-twins-dead-father-says-i-often-wonder-why-we-moved-to-poonch-10676774/) cites **district government data** on the shelling of May 7–8:

- **14 civilian fatalities**, including two school-aged twins described through first-person family interview. This is a subregional death count, **not** the all-India total and **not a new 14 to add** to the separate India-wide shelling figures.
- **65 persons injured**, **598 damaged homes** by the quoted district data.
- Governments reportedly announced a **₹16 lakh per-death family ex-gratia framework**, **₹1 lakh for serious injuries**, and **₹5 lakh extra for permanent disabilities**, with eligibility and combinations not independently audited.
- Official figures **reported by the journalist** specify **₹7.39 crore disbursed for pucca-house reconstruction** and **₹2.25 crore for kuccha houses**. The underlying allocation order, beneficiary/payment ledger, accounting period and overlapping programme definitions have **not** been obtained. Consequently record as **reported government expenditure** and not a personally reconciled audit.
- Officials reportedly proposed **200–300 prefabricated homes** for people unable to restore property, and a separate **5,693 new underground bunkers**. Both are **proposals**, not completed units; press and earlier provincial statements corroborate the status.
- An individual interviewed family described receiving **₹14 lakh** in ex-gratia and one spouse receiving a public appointment, while the announced general death award was **₹16 lakh**. **Do not call it official underpayment without the family-specific award, date, employer rules and whether other components were paid later.**

**Do not mix three different housing denominators:** (a) Poonch 598, district and category per government data quoted in May 2026; (b) the Ministry of Home Affairs **2,060 houses** covered by *additional* relief across border districts in June 2025; (c) **~500 Uri damaged houses** described in May 2026 press reporting. They overlap or differ in geography/date/eligibility and cannot be summed. A June 2025 Tribune article's claim of **over 2,000 houses damaged in Poonch** may use broader assessed damaged structures, preliminary claims, district-versus-town scope or another date; it is not reconciled with the later government-attributed 598 figure. Obtain original category definitions instead of assuming false reporting.

**Survivor evidence:** Reports from Poonch, Uri and Bathinda establish serious persistent consequences. Narratives and named interviews are directly relevant but cannot support an invented universal restitution rate or preempt a beneficiary audit.

## 4. Claims about built versus planned border shelters

The [November 21, 2025 J&K Lieutenant Governor statement reported by PTI](https://www.rediff.com/news/commentary/2025/nov/21/proposal-for-5693-additional-bunkers-in-poonch-submitted-for-nod-jk-lg/06dcfa3cb7e838f556b5930037da678e) said **1,125 bunkers had been completed in Poonch over preceding years**, and **5,693 additional bunkers had been proposed**. Separately, a launch for **521 new homes** in Poonch/Rajouri served **combined natural-calamity and Pakistani-shelling affected households**. **Do not treat all 521 as Operation Sindoor-specific homes.**

[February 11, 2026 Times of India follow-up](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/india/no-bunkers-built-in-border-areas-even-10-months-after-op-sindoor/articleshow/128172472.cms) quoted local legislators complaining of lack of post-May 2025 new bunker construction. The prior 1,125 historical built bunkers **do not negate** a later complaint that the **additional** shelter proposal remained incomplete. The July 2026 locality rollout/current national completion status is not authenticated in this pass.

**Finding:** Sources support both historic constructed shelters and a significant *unimplemented proposed expansion* as of the cited reporting dates. They do not prove that zero bunkers existed or that 5,693 had already been finished.

## 5. Independent coding perspective: ACLED's measured events are not battle-damage counts

[ACLED June 2025 Asia-Pacific overview](https://acleddata.com/update/asia-pacific-overview-june-2025) recounts the May 7–10 escalation and says ACLED **recorded eight Pakistani airstrikes on Indian territory and 11 Indian airstrikes against mainly PAF facilities** during the later phase. These are **database-coded event categories** according to ACLED's reporting/methodology, not the number of aircraft destroyed or the total missiles launched. ACLED states that most drone/missile attacks were intercepted. Its 8/11 are not a symmetrical independent victory score and do not prove any particular site damage or legal compliance.

**Next task:** Acquire the dated ACLED events with unique IDs and codebook, reconcile strike events with geographical footprints and ISPR/MEA claims. One reporting event can include multiple projectiles; physical hits require separate imagery and ground records.
