# Pakistan's May 2025 counteroperation: independent documentary review

Research checkpoint 03. Cutoff 19 October 2026.

## Scope and source standard

Pakistan's publicly named Operation Bunyan-ul-Marsoos was announced on 10 May 2025. Pakistan also used the name Marka-e-Haq for the broader crisis dating from the 22 April Pahalgam attack. This timeline designation is not evidence of nineteen days of direct military combat.

Pakistan's military spokesperson said in a briefing after the fighting that its operation had engaged 26 Indian targets. That establishes a dated official claim, not twenty-six independently verified impacts. The original statement is summarized by [Radio Pakistan, 12 May](https://www.radio.gov.pk/12-05-2025/armed-forces-honour-pledge-to-nation-against-indian-aggression-dg-ispr).

The [Stimson Center's public evidence review, 28 May](https://www.stimson.org/2025/four-days-in-may-the-india-pakistan-crisis-of-2025/) found little independently observable damage from Pakistan's later attacks, while stressing the limits of commercial satellite imagery. Crucially, India's own [10 May military briefing](https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2025/05/mil-250510-india-meab01.htm) acknowledged some limited damage to personnel and equipment. This establishes that the correct finding is neither total Pakistani success nor zero damage.

## Claim assessments

| Proposition | Evidence-based status |
|---|---|
| Pakistan announced a counteroperation on 10 May | Verified |
| Pakistan publicly claimed to have engaged 26 military targets | Verified as a statement |
| All 26 were seriously damaged | Unsupported |
| Some Indian military personnel or equipment suffered limited damage | India acknowledged this publicly |
| A major Indian air-defence system was conclusively destroyed | Unsupported by independently examined evidence |
| Pakistan disabled 70% of India's electricity grid | Unsupported; contradicted by an Indian official fact-check and uncorroborated by outage data reviewed |
| Pakistan used missiles and drones | Supported by state reporting and contemporaneous accounts |
| The consequences decisively settled future deterrence | Not presently verifiable |

The striking [70% electricity-grid claim](https://www.radio.gov.pk/10-05-2025/pakistan-launches-operation-bunyan-un-marsoos-to-respond-indian-aggression) was rejected by [India's PIB fact-check](https://newsonair.gov.in/hi/%E0%A4%B8%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%95%E0%A4%BE%E0%A4%B0-%E0%A4%A8%E0%A5%87-%E0%A4%B8%E0%A5%8B%E0%A4%B6%E0%A4%B2-%E0%A4%AE%E0%A5%80%E0%A4%A1%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AF%E0%A4%BE-%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%87-%E0%A4%AD%E0%A4%BE/). There is no corroborated public record of a nationwide grid failure of the stated magnitude. That does not exclude smaller unrelated cyber incidents.

## Apply exactly the same test to India

The [Washington Post's 14 May independent satellite investigation](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/14/india-pakistan-strikes-conflict-damage/) found physical damage at multiple Pakistani airfields but did not establish the loss of every aircraft or wholesale operational paralysis. One [Indian retrospective PIB note](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?ModuleId=3&NoteId=154451&lang=2&reg=48) asserted that a large fraction of Pakistan's air force infrastructure was destroyed without releasing a comparable independent denominator. Both governments' sweeping destruction claims require independent facility-level accounting.

## Evidentiary limits

No original commercial imagery products, verified equipment loss inventory, audited casualty ledger or independently attributable nationwide cyber forensics were acquired in this research pass. Published imagery from independent journalists is treated as independent *reporting*, not as raw images authenticated anew by this project.

## Next research actions

Document historic non-sensitive outcome evidence, original claims and later corrections. Distinguish announcements of attempted strikes from observed physical effects. Never infer that equipment in an affected area was destroyed simply because an installation was targeted. Current dispositions, exploitable defensive configurations and tactical maps are out of scope.
