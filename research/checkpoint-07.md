# Operation Sindoor: investigative checkpoint 07

**Cutoff:** 19 October 2026  
**Continuity:** Substantive successor to [checkpoint 06](checkpoint-06.md); prior findings retained unless explicitly qualified.  
**Status:** Independent incomplete public-record investigation. No claim of original witness interviews, complete military flight logs, fresh satellite-pixel authentication or finished casualty audit.

## Executive findings

1. A **9 June 2025 original Ministry of Home Affairs release** establishes India's *reported* evacuation/relief quantities: **325,000 evacuated**, **around 15,000 accommodated in approximately 397 designated shelter facilities**, additional compensation provision of **₹25 crore for 2,060 houses**, **394 ambulances**, and **2,818 civil defence volunteers**. These figures anchor the scale of the civilian response and cannot be conflated with unique killed/injured counts or final documented disbursements.
2. A **8 May 2026 field report from Uri** identifies people still living in temporary circumstances and describes a specific family receiving **₹1.5 lakh**, insufficient for their home's full rebuilding by the interview date. It reports approximately **500 damaged houses** in the area. This is a significant lived-impact follow-up, but **not** a statewide audit and **not an additive count** to the ministry's 2,060 houses.
3. **UN Security Council S/2025/315**, a Pakistan letter dated **20 May 2025**, authenticates Pakistan's **denials** of claims that it used a Shaheen missile and deliberately targeted the **Golden Temple in Amritsar**. The originating Indian Golden Temple allegation is also contemporaneously authenticated as a 19 May Indian Army officer's statement. Neither side's claim conclusively determines missile inventory, intercept location, target intention or authenticity of an allegedly deleted video. This is a **dispute**, not an adjudicated fact.
4. A **30 July 2025 Pakistani statement circulated as UN S/2025/494** documents Pakistan's rejection of India's July Lok Sabha account, including its scepticism toward **Operation Mahadev**, its reference to third-party ceasefire roles, and its own victory statements. It authenticates the political counter-narrative **not the factual truth** of any allegations about the suspects, missions or aircraft.
5. New **October 2026 reporting** describes tests of remotely activated, geo-targeted civil-defence sirens. A separate **2 May 2026 Indian government launch** establishes an operational national cell-broadcast alert capability. The two are distinct: successful phone-alert testing does **not** mean the standardized siren system has already been deployed nationwide.

## 1. Indian civilian harm: official evacuation and housing records

**Primary source:** [Ministry of Home Affairs, Press Information Bureau, 9 June 2025, PRID 2135226](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2135226&lang=2&reg=48). The document says:

| Quantity / measure | Exact reported scope | Evidence classification |
|---|---|---|
| **325,000** people evacuated | Border districts of Jammu and Kashmir after cross-border firing | Verified as ministry's stated aggregate; person-level deduplication unavailable |
| **~15,000** sheltered | Around **397** shelter sheds/accommodation centres | Subset of recorded evacuees; no basis to classify every other evacuee as homeless or unsupported |
| **2,060** damaged homes covered | Additional **₹25 crore** provision for compensation | Scope of relief allocation, not independently inspected house-by-house inventory or actual payment ledger |
| **₹2 lakh** fully damaged; **₹1 lakh** partially damaged | Additional announced per-house compensation schedule | Announced supplemental rates; do not mistake for total family receipt, replacement cost or disbursement |
| **394** ambulances | Deployed across J&K affected districts; **62** in Poonch | Emergency capacity deployment, not number of injured |
| **2,818** civil defence volunteers | Deployed for health, emergency, livestock and supplies | Staff mobilization, not victims or evacuees |

The release also says commercial, religious and school properties were struck or damaged and that compensation/appointments were provided to affected families, without a verifiable victim and payments list. Note that "additional allocation for homes" does **not** denote all humanitarian government expenditure. The ₹25 crore allocation should not be naively multiplied again as a complete realized loss.

**Inferential guardrails:** Shelter facility occupancy is a recorded **subset** of evacuees, not a binary adequate/inadequate accommodation measure; the same person may move through several arrangements. Applying ₹1–2 lakh to 2,060 houses produces only a rough range without actual full/partial counts; other claims, local grant schedules, and payment dates may differ. No verified achieved rebuilding rate follows from the release.

### Follow-up from Uri, May 2026

[New Indian Express, 8 May 2026, Uri follow-up](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2026/May/08/operation-sindoor-uri-survivors-still-struggle-to-rebuild-lives) interviews persons with homes damaged by the 2025 shelling. It reports about **500 damaged houses** around the locality, one civilian killed and more than a dozen injured, and features one family reporting receipt of **₹1.5 lakh**, insufficient to rebuild the home by May 2026. Children reportedly faced interrupted educational access.

**Assessment:** Independent reportage supports persistence of *some* hardship a year later and particular compensatory shortfalls. The reporter's local **500** homes should **not** be added to the ministry's **2,060** total without matching localities, dates and assessment categories. The compensation sum reported to a family cannot be presumed to be the sole eligible payment or the statutory rate for all fully damaged homes.

**Next record request:** J&K/Punjab district-wise damage registers, assessment numbers, full/partial classifications, bank/payment issuance ledgers, grievances and appeal outcomes, reconstruction certificates, public institutional repair, and independent civil-society sampling.

## 2. S/2025/315: contested Golden Temple and Shaheen missile allegations

**Original UN letter:** [S/2025/315, letter dated 20 May, formally distributed 21 May 2025](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082947/files/S_2025_315-EN.pdf), signed by Pakistan Permanent Representative Asim Iftikhar Ahmad, transmitting Pakistan Foreign Ministry statements of 19 and 20 May.

- **Shaheen missile:** Pakistan denied having fired a Shaheen-series missile in Operation Bunyan-ul-Marsoos and alleged an Indian Army social-media post/video had been removed after publication. [Pakistan's own contemporaneous 19 May release](https://mofa.gov.pk/press-releases/statement-by-the-spokesperson-in-response-to-indian-medias-baseless-claims-regarding-pakistans-use-of-shaheen-missile-in-operation-bunyanun-marsoos-bm) documents this accusation. No authentic archived original military post, platform deletion audit, radar track or weapons fragment chain has been obtained by this investigation.
- **Golden Temple:** India's [19 May 2025 reporting on Maj Gen Kartik C. Seshadri's public briefing](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/chandigarh/golden-temple-pakistan-india-strikes-air-defence-drone-10015480/) establishes a senior Indian Army officer *said* Pakistan had tried to target the Golden Temple on the night of 7–8 May. [Pakistan's 20 May original state statement](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-rejects-indian-allegations-of-attempt-to-attack-golden-temple/) denied it. A video of incoming weapons, without a verified trajectory, target programme and intent, cannot alone establish the target as the Golden Temple itself.

**Provisional result:** Competing official assertions authenticated; particular target intention and Shaheen use unresolved. A claimed post deletion is itself unverified, not a fact simply because a ministry repeated it in UN correspondence. The claims belong in the conflict's disinformation register with explicit provenance.

## 3. Pakistan's late-July parliamentary counterclaim enters UN record

**Document:** [S/2025/494, 30 July 2025 letter and Pakistan spokesman annex](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4086444/files/S_2025_494-EN.pdf). The annex:
- rejects the Indian Lok Sabha government's claims about Operation Sindoor's success and the Pahalgam inquiry;
- challenges the credibility of the alleged Operation Mahadev suspect identifications;
- asserts Pakistani aircraft and military operational success;
- alleges active roles for third parties in the ceasefire;
- condemns India's Indus Waters Treaty abeyance position.

**Method:** These are a formally circulated **interested-state denial and narrative**; UN distribution confirms document provenance, **not** judicial or factual endorsement by the United Nations. It neither overturns the NIA's December 2025 prosecution nor verifies Indian aircraft and Pakistani target totals. Compare its allegations with the July Indian parliamentary original speech, NIA original court filings, aircraft-loss dossier and the later August 2026 separate Indus Waters award. Do not count this July response as a second independent source for Pakistan's earlier repeated claims.

## 4. October 2026: civil-defence warning reforms versus fielded capability

[Indian Express, 15 October 2026](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/air-raid-sirens-back-after-operation-sindoor-10922127/) reports that tests in Jaisalmer (**30 September**), Bihar (**6 October**) and Mumbai (**8–9 October**) are examining a centrally triggered, geo-targeted siren prototype. Its account cites an on-site Maharashtra civil-defence note and local officials describing non-standard state purchases of air-raid sirens in the 2025 emergency.

**Primary independent official milestone:** [Indian Department of Telecommunications/PIB announcement, 2 May 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2257499&lang=2&reg=3) establishes launch and nationwide testing of an indigenous **Cell Broadcast System (CBS)** for mobile emergency warnings, developed with C-DOT and NDMA. This is not itself a claim that all installed **physical sirens** support the new central activation scheme.

**Assessment:** Post-conflict institutional learning is visible in documented investment/testing and operational trials. Do **not** infer that India's entire siren infrastructure is standardized or that prototypes have passed all reliability/environmental tests. An early warning system's successful demonstration is not evidence that civil-defence vulnerabilities are fully resolved. [NDMA SACHET national alert portal](https://sachet.ndma.gov.in/) is a broader hazard alert infrastructure and not a 2025 war-damage measure.

## 5. OHCHR response investigation: negative results bounded

The OHCHR Special Procedures public [IND 8/2025 case view](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TmSearch/RelCom?code=IND+8%2F2025) and [combined IND 10/2025 / PAK 10/2025 case view](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TmSearch/RelCom?code=IND+10%2F2025) were revisited on 19 October 2026. Their visible **Replies received** column showed **no linked response** in any of the three cases. The portal demonstrably displays dated replies for other cases elsewhere.

**Bounded finding:** No replies were **linked on those queried public case rows at access time**. This does not establish that either government sent no reply via private diplomatic channels or has made no relevant public statement elsewhere. No signed India/Pakistan response specifically matching those OHCHR reference numbers was located in the targeted public search. The original allegations remain allegations and cannot be reclassified as proven because of a blank reply field.

## 6. India's Article 51 letter, aircraft and civilian fatalities remain unresolved

A targeted search for an Indian formal Security Council communication reporting May 2025 defensive measures under **UN Charter Article 51** did not identify a matching primary document. The 16 October 2025 OHCHR [AL IND 10/2025](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadPublicCommunicationFile?gId=30361) independently records the expert signatories' view that India had not notified the Council. **Do not convert non-retrieval to a conclusive universal assertion**: comprehensive UN document-symbol and permanent-mission archival access remains open.

The existing [structured casualty register](../data/research.json) records a *dated Indian-government-attributed minimum of 16 civilian deaths as of May 2025*. Do not add this to newer 2025 news daily cumulative figures. Independent civilian hospital, burial, disability, injury and district/state records remain uncompiled. Official reported housing/displacement numbers here are **not fatalities**. The airframe-count dispute likewise remains as in [the aircraft dossier](aircraft-loss-review.md), with the need for serial-specific confirmation from both sides.
