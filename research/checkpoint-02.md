**Completed:** 19 October 2026  
**Research cutoff:** 19 October 2026  
**Status:** Substantive expansion and correction of checkpoint 01. Investigation remains incomplete and no background task is scheduled.

## Executive findings by evidentiary tier

### 1. Indian fighter losses: expanded independent corroboration

- **High-confidence supported finding:** Indian initial aircraft losses occurred. This is consistent with [CDS Anil Chauhan's 31 May Reuters interview](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/india-says-changed-tactics-worked-well-conflict-with-pakistan-2025-05-31/).
- **High-confidence supported finding:** At least one Indian Rafale was lost. The [Washington Post visual investigation of 9 May](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/09/fighter-jets-india-pakistan-attack/) found Rafale-consistent wreckage, and [French Air Force Chief Jérôme Bellanger's 9 July 2025 parliamentary testimony](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/opendata/CRCANR5L17S2025PO59046N082.html) explicitly referred to an Indian Rafale being shot down. The latter is original French official testimony, not a social-media claim.
- **Medium-confidence supported finding:** Visual evidence is also consistent with a Mirage 2000 crash. The Washington Post could not establish the *cause* of its loss. A possible third crash site lacks aircraft identification.
- **Not established:** Pakistan's five/six Indian fighter totals; identification of all claimed aircraft, pilot fates, missile-to-crash causal chain, or universal comparative superiority of one aircraft manufacturer.

### 2. Pakistani aircraft: equal evidentiary standard

India's Air Chief Marshal A.P. Singh [claimed on 9 August 2025](https://newsonair.gov.in/india-downed-five-pakistani-fighter-jets-during-operation-sindoor-air-chief-marshal-a-p-singh/) that five Pakistani fighter aircraft and one larger military platform had been shot down. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/india-shot-down-six-pakistani-military-aircraft-may-air-force-chief-says-2025-08-09/) reported Pakistani Defence Minister Khawaja Asif rejected those claims. The Indian chief's [October 3 subsequent remarks](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/pakistani-fighter-jets-f-16s-destroyed-operation-sindoor-air-force-chief-10285337/) add claims of aircraft damage/destruction on the ground.

**Finding:** The statements are publicly verified; a unique aircraft-by-aircraft loss ledger is not. Never combine August claimed airborne interceptions with October claimed ground effects without excluding duplicate aircraft and distinguishing destroyed versus damaged.

### 3. Pakistani airfield damage

[Washington Post, 14 May](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/14/india-pakistan-strikes-conflict-damage/) reports satellite/video evidence of damage to **at least six Pakistani airfields**, including three hangars, two runways and two mobile buildings. A Pakistani military spokesperson confirmed hits at five military bases and one civilian airport. Expert assessments agree visible damage was meaningful but diverge on effectiveness; one found bases not disabled.

**Finding:** Airfield physical damage is strongly supported. Loss of a particular Pakistani aircraft inside a hangar, sustained loss of operational capacity or decisive effect on ceasefire diplomacy is not thereby proven.

### 4. Pahalgam investigation, 2026 developments

[Indian Express, 25 June 2026](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/pahalgam-terror-attack-nia-chargesheet-pakistan-based-handler-10756041/) reported NIA prosecution material including maps dated 15–16 April 2025, alleged handler communication and recovered phone evidence. This increases specificity of the prosecution case but is not equivalent to independent authentication of device data.

[Indian Express, 4 March 2026](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/nia-says-gopro-connected-to-pahalgam-attack-was-activated-in-china-court-seeks-assistance-from-chinese-authorities-10563786/) reports a court-approved judicial assistance request to China regarding a GoPro camera's sales/activation chain. **A commercial device linkage to China does not establish Chinese government complicity**. The request and any returned evidence still require review.

The original 1,597-page prosecution chargesheet and full supplementary filing have **not** been acquired. Individual guilt and state involvement beyond public evidence remain allegations, not verdicts.

### 5. Casualties: provisional dated reporting, not a verified final total

[Pakistan's ISPR statement, APP, 13 May](https://www.app.com.pk/national/40-civilians-11-troops-martyred-in-indian-aggression-ispr/) reported **40 civilians and 11 military personnel killed** and **121 civilians and 78 service personnel injured**. A [Reuters article, 13 May](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistan-says-clashes-with-neighbour-india-killed-more-than-50-2025-05-13/) reported India had said at least **16 civilians and 5 military personnel** died.

These official figures describe at least 72 deaths in the two countries if combined without population/time overlap, but **72 is not a verified deduplicated final toll**. No comprehensive individual record or full injury count has been obtained. A contradiction exists because Pakistan's 13 May military statement lists **five** Air Force fatalities while a [Washington Post report, 14 May](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/14/india-pakistan-strikes-conflict-damage/) attributes **six** to the military. Source versions require reconciliation.

### 6. India’s parliamentary no-casualty wording versus the May military figure

[PIB's account of Rajnath Singh's 28 July Lok Sabha remarks](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2149356&lang=2&reg=48) says that no Indian soldiers suffered casualties **during the operation**. Yet [AP's account of India's 11 May joint military briefing](https://apnews.com/article/6fa80010fa8ffee1da1f28e7b593aa2d) records **five Indian soldiers killed** in the fighting.

**Status:** This is a real documentary wording tension, not a demonstrated deliberate falsehood. The phrase “during the operation” may mean India's initial counterterrorism strikes or a narrower phase, whereas the five deaths may concern broader 7–10 May fighting. Resolving the issue requires full unedited official statements and date/incident classification for the five reported fatalities. Both statements are kept in the contradiction register, X18.

### 7. Cessation and its immediate aftermath

A documented cessation took effect at 17:00 IST (11:30 UTC) on 10 May. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/india/pakistan-says-three-air-bases-targeted-by-indian-missiles-2025-05-10/) recorded post-cessation fire and explosions, with both governments accusing the other of violations. This falsifies any description of the first implementation hours as incident-free but does **not** independently assign responsibility.

### 8. Forewarning chronology unresolved

In [official French parliamentary testimony](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/opendata/CRCANR5L17S2025PO59046N082.html), Bellanger said his Indian counterpart explained having warned Pakistan of an imminent raid, reflecting escalation management. India's [17 May MEA clarification](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/mea-rejects-claims-that-india-informed-pakistan-before-launching-operation-sindoor-clarifies-on-jaishankars-statement/articleshow/121238111.cms) rejects notice **before launch**, while the [Prime Minister's 29 July parliamentary account](https://www.pmindia.gov.in/en/news_updates/pm-addressing-lok-sabha-during-special-discussion-on-operation-sindoor/) said notification followed the initial objectives.

These statements differ and need exact, authenticated DGMO timeline. No inference about deliberate compromise, advance Pakistani targeting advantage or misconduct is currently supported.

### 9. Foreign influence and misinformation

[Associated Press, 6 July 2025](https://apnews.com/article/64eec86b6e89718d6a49d8fdedf565f4) described French intelligence findings alleging Chinese diplomatic efforts to damage Rafale sales. The report should be credited as a sourced French intelligence account, not an independently reproducible state-influence finding.

[Soch Fact Check, 31 May](https://www.sochfactcheck.com/reuters-did-not-report-pakistan-downed-42-indian-iaf-dassault-aviation-rafale-jets/) documented a fabricated purported Reuters card saying 42 Rafales were downed. **Contradicted by evidence, high confidence**. This does not discredit Reuters' *real* aircraft-loss reporting.
