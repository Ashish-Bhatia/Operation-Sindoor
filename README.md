## Evidence-led findings, checkpoint 02

- The 22 April Pahalgam attack killed 26 people, as recorded by the [UN Security Council](https://press.un.org/en/2025/sc16050.doc.htm).
- India's [first contemporaneous release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2127370), timestamped 7 May at 01:44 IST, reported nine targeted sites and no Pakistani military facilities targeted in the opening action. This is verification of the **release**, not all its operational claims.
- India's Chief of Defence Staff acknowledged initial air losses in [Reuters' 31 May interview](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/india-says-changed-tactics-worked-well-conflict-with-pakistan-2025-05-31/), without specifying a total or aircraft types.
- The US administration claimed a role in facilitating the 10 May cessation, while India emphasized bilateral military contact. The causal issue remains disputed.
- A [Washington Post crash-image investigation](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/09/fighter-jets-india-pakistan-attack/) and [July 9 French parliamentary testimony](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/opendata/CRCANR5L17S2025PO59046N082.html) support the loss of at least one Indian Rafale. Evidence of a Mirage 2000 loss is also substantial but not fully established. Neither source establishes every asserted aircraft or weapon attribution.
- The [Washington Post's historical imagery analysis](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/14/india-pakistan-strikes-conflict-damage/) documented damaged structures/runways at six Pakistani airfields, not necessarily destroyed aircraft or disabled bases.
- In August 2025, India's air chief [claimed six Pakistani aircraft shot down](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/india-shot-down-six-pakistani-military-aircraft-may-air-force-chief-says-2025-08-09/). Pakistan denied it; independent hull-by-hull verification is outstanding.
- [Pakistan's 13 May military report](https://www.app.com.pk/national/40-civilians-11-troops-martyred-in-indian-aggression-ispr/) listed 40 civilians and 11 military personnel killed. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistan-says-clashes-with-neighbour-india-killed-more-than-50-2025-05-13/) attributed at least 16 civilians and five military killed to India's then-current statements. These are dated provisional official figures, not an independent total.
- NIA subsequently announced chargesheets in [December 2025](https://nia.gov.in/nia-chargesheets-pak-based-lettrf-6-other-accused-pahalgam-terror-attack-case) and [July 2026](https://nia.gov.in/pak-based-lettrf-chief-hafiz-saeed-named-nia-supplementary-chargesheet-pahalgam-terror-attack). Prosecutorial allegations are not judicial findings of guilt.

## How to use the website

The single-page site has a mobile hamburger menu, chronological incident files, the nine initial targets, aircraft and ceasefire disputes, searchable claim cards, a contradiction register, unresolved questions and a searchable original-source library.

It uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build tool, external JavaScript framework, analytics or tracking cookies. All factual database records load from `data/research.json`.

### GitHub Pages configuration

If GitHub Pages is not enabled, go to **Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Deploy from a branch → main / (root) → Save**. Pages hosting has not been independently verified at this checkpoint.

## Data standard

Every item is attributed by source ID and categorized by **type**, **review status**, **classification**, **confidence** and/or **evidence gap** as applicable. The structured registry currently contains a 39-event chronology, 54 assessed claims, nine initial target-site records, 18 contradictions, 25 open questions, four dated cumulative casualty records and 58 source entries. Source URL repetitions are flagged and not counted as independent corroboration. It includes a deliberately marked repeated source reference; repeated references are **not** independent corroborations.

Do not equate:
- an official statement with independent confirmation of its substance,
- a chargesheet with a conviction,
- a satellite image of damage with proof of casualties or military-system destruction,
- a lost aircraft with general comparative superiority of the systems involved,
- an unresolved claim with a discredited claim.

Do not use present-day exploitable security vulnerabilities or private individual information in contributions.

## Editorial corrections

Every substantive change should cite original evidence, identify the prior statement, explain the correction and update the revision history in `research/checkpoint-02.md` or a later checkpoint. Uncertainty is published rather than filled in.

## Current limitations

This edition has **not** retrieved and independently reviewed complete NIA filed exhibits, confidential intelligence, original licensed imagery, comprehensive medical/casualty records, every military-briefing recording or the latest signed court orders. Comprehensive historical, treaty/legal, humanitarian and economic reconciliation remains incomplete.

The October 2026 revision additionally compares the 7 May opening objectives and 8 May escalation claims with the July parliamentary account. It also covers the disputed August 2026 Indus Waters Treaty arbitral award and distinguishes Air India's conditional aviation-loss forecast from audited realized costs.

The repository is independent and not affiliated with either government, armed forces, any media organization or OpenAI.
