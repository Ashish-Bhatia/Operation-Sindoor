## Evidence-led findings, checkpoints 02–09

- The 22 April Pahalgam attack killed 26 people, as recorded by the [UN Security Council](https://press.un.org/en/2025/sc16050.doc.htm).
- India's [first contemporaneous release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2127370), timestamped 7 May at 01:44 IST, reported nine targeted sites and no Pakistani military facilities targeted in the opening action. This is verification of the **release**, not all its operational claims.
- India's Chief of Defence Staff acknowledged initial air losses in [Reuters' 31 May interview](https://www.reuters.com/business/aerospace-defense/india-says-changed-tactics-worked-well-conflict-with-pakistan-2025-05-31/), without specifying a total or aircraft types.
- The US administration claimed a role in facilitating the 10 May cessation, while India emphasized bilateral military contact. The original 12 May Indian PIB explainer itself says Pakistan approached the US to broker calm, a narrower assertion than US decisiveness. The causal issue remains disputed.
- A [Washington Post crash-image investigation](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/09/fighter-jets-india-pakistan-attack/) and [July 9 French parliamentary testimony](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/opendata/CRCANR5L17S2025PO59046N082.html) support the loss of at least one Indian Rafale. Evidence of a Mirage 2000 loss is also substantial but not fully established. Neither source establishes every asserted aircraft or weapon attribution.
- The [Washington Post's historical imagery analysis](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2025/05/14/india-pakistan-strikes-conflict-damage/) documented damaged structures/runways at six Pakistani airfields, not necessarily destroyed aircraft or disabled bases.
- Pakistan said its 10 May counteroperation engaged 26 Indian military targets, but independent imagery analysis does not support the implied comprehensive destruction. India itself acknowledged limited harm at four air installations. The purported 70% Indian power-grid cyber collapse lacks independent corroboration.
- The 10 May DGMO call is publicly dated to **15:35 IST** and cessation to **17:00 IST**. The May 12 follow-up and May 18 no-expiry clarification are documented.
- In August 2025, India's air chief [claimed six Pakistani aircraft shot down](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/india-shot-down-six-pakistani-military-aircraft-may-air-force-chief-says-2025-08-09/). Pakistan denied it; independent hull-by-hull verification is outstanding.
- [Pakistan's 13 May military report](https://www.app.com.pk/national/40-civilians-11-troops-martyred-in-indian-aggression-ispr/) listed 40 civilians and 11 military personnel killed. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/pakistan-says-clashes-with-neighbour-india-killed-more-than-50-2025-05-13/) attributed at least 16 civilians and five military killed to India's then-current statements. These are dated provisional official figures, not an independent total.
- NIA subsequently announced chargesheets in [December 2025](https://nia.gov.in/nia-chargesheets-pak-based-lettrf-6-other-accused-pahalgam-terror-attack-case) and [July 2026](https://nia.gov.in/pak-based-lettrf-chief-hafiz-saeed-named-nia-supplementary-chargesheet-pahalgam-terror-attack). Prosecutorial allegations are not judicial findings of guilt.
- A [25 November 2025 French Defence Ministry reply](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/questions/QANR5L17QE6745) referred to the presumed loss of an Indian Rafale, but stated that the circumstances remained unclear and a claimed SPECTRA failure was undocumented.
- A [June 2026 IAF 36-Rafale support RFP report](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/air-force-iaf-document-36-rafales-pakistan-operation-sindoor-loss-claims-2932499-2026-06-23) does not itself constitute a serial-numbered survival inventory.
- [May](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/story/operation-sindoor-pakistan-airbase-repairs-still-unfinished-after-a-year-satellite-images-show-2907688-2026-05-06) and [September 2026](https://www.indiatoday.in/india/interactive/story/490-days-satellite-images-show-brahmos-crater-at-sargodha-reopened-for-repair-2997799-2026-09-18) satellite reporting documents repairs at damaged bases, not continuous inoperability or exact aircraft kills.
- [UN report S/2025/482](https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2127961/n2517600.pdf) describes five Pahalgam attackers, while later NIA prosecution refers to three principal gunmen. The discrepancy is not yet reconciled.
- The [31 August 2026 Indus Waters award](https://pca.int/en/news/pca-press-release-pca-case-no-2023-01-the-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-islamic-republic-of-pakistan-v-republic-of-india-6/) and [India's express rejection](https://eoibelgrade.gov.in/index.php/listview/NzE2) leave jurisdiction and implementation disputed.
- The **UN's five / NIA's three attacker-count difference** requires a temporal correction: the [UN report's information cutoff](https://www.ecoi.net/en/file/local/2127961/n2517600.pdf) was **22 June 2025**, and the [NIA clarification](https://nia.gov.in/update-investigations-pehalgam-case-and-clarification-regarding-speculationsonpahalgamterror-attack) was issued **23 June**. Neither count is independently verified through full filed exhibits.
- India's [Defence Ministry disavowed a circulating Services-attributed Pahalgam suspect report](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2152335&lang=2&reg=3) on 4 August 2025. An [October 2026 NIA LeT property attachment](https://nia.gov.in/nia-attaches-property-used-harbour-pakistani-let-terrorists-srinagar) has a **different 2026 case number**, not a demonstrated Pahalgam case connection.
- The [31 August 2026 treaty award's reproduced text](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-pakistan-v-india-award-on-the-status-of-the-indus-waters-treaty-in-respect-of-india-s-decision-on-abeyance-monday-31st-august-2026) explicitly acknowledges May 2025 combat, does not rule on Pahalgam attribution and addresses whether armed conflict existed **at the time of its ruling**. Its [Ratle interim order](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-indus-waters-western-rivers-arbitration-pakistan-v-india-order-on-the-interim-measures-application-of-pakistan-dated-4-march-2026-monday-31st-august-2026) restricts specified works, not all construction. India rejects the tribunal's competence.
- Original UN letters [S/2025/289](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4081956/files/S_2025_289-EN.pdf) and [S/2025/290](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082002/files/S_2025_290-EN.pdf) document Pakistan's **Article 51 assertions** as early as 6–7 May; [S/2025/308](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082687/files/S_2025_308-EN.pdf) reports the completed counteroperation. **Notification is not a legal verdict or independent proof of shootdowns.**
- [Pakistan's 13 May UN letter](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082311/files/S_2025_305-EN.pdf) credits several friendly countries with sending de-escalation messages, while [India's official 4 December parliamentary answer](https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/269/AU554_Fdrh9b.pdf?source=pqars) states the cessation understanding was arrived at directly between DGMOs. The statements describe distinct possible stages.
- Independent UN Special Procedures experts sent [AL IND 10/2025](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadPublicCommunicationFile?gId=30361) and [AL PAK 10/2025](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadPublicCommunicationFile?gId=30432), raising concerns about **both states' strike conduct**; a separate [AL IND 8/2025](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadPublicCommunicationFile?gId=30287) recites **unverified allegations** about post-Pahalgam arrests. They are requests for explanations, **not judicial findings**.
- India's [9 June 2025 Ministry of Home Affairs release](https://www.pib.gov.in/Pressreleaseshare.aspx?PRID=2135226&lang=2&reg=48) reported **325,000 evacuated**, approximately **15,000 in designated shelters**, and additional **₹25 crore compensation for 2,060 damaged houses**. These are official stated totals, not validated disbursements or fatality counts.
- A [May 2026 Uri follow-up](https://www.newindianexpress.com/nation/2026/May/08/operation-sindoor-uri-survivors-still-struggle-to-rebuild-lives) documented some homes still unreconstructed and compensation hardship. Local damaged-house counts are potentially a subset of the ministry figures.
- [Pakistan's UN S/2025/315](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4082947/files/S_2025_315-EN.pdf) disputed a **Golden Temple target-intention allegation** and **Shaheen missile use**; [S/2025/494](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/4086444/files/S_2025_494-EN.pdf) formally transmitted Pakistan's July rebuttal of India's Operation Sindoor/Mahadev assertions. UN distribution verifies the statements, not their correctness.
- India's [May 2026 national mobile cell-broadcast launch](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2257499&lang=2&reg=3) is a separate milestone from [October 2026 prototype tests of remotely activated physical air-raid sirens](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/air-raid-sirens-back-after-operation-sindoor-10922127/). Nationwide siren rollout remains unverified.
- **Pakistan revised its reported service deaths from 11 on 13 May to [13 on 14 May 2025](https://www.radio.gov.pk/14-05-2025/two-more-valiant-sons-of-soil-embraced-shahadat-today)** after two previously wounded personnel died, one each from the Army and Air Force. These are overlapping cumulative reports, not additive figures.
- A [May 2026 Bathinda investigation](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/chandigarh/he-died-saving-others-op-sindoor-aircraft-crash-victims-family-in-bathinda-awaits-job-one-year-after-death-10679806/) identifies **two civilian deaths** arising from the May 7 aircraft crash, including a person who died days later. The aircraft type, combat causation and national-shelling-count overlap remain unconfirmed.
- [Poonch anniversary reporting](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/operation-sindoor-a-year-later-twins-dead-father-says-i-often-wonder-why-we-moved-to-poonch-10676774/) cites district-government figures of **14 civilians killed, 65 injured, 598 houses damaged**, plus **₹7.39 crore** pucca and **₹2.25 crore** kuccha housing disbursements. The original district accounts and link to the earlier MHA ₹25-crore provision remain unavailable.
- [Sindh's government account](https://cm.sindh.gov.pk/news/murad-announces-compensation-for-martyrs-killed-and-injured-during-the-india-attack) named six PAF deaths associated with Bholari. Personnel deaths do **not** establish how many military aircraft were destroyed.
- **India's May-to-June casualty reconciliation:** six [original National War Memorial entries](https://nationalwarmemorial.gov.in/) establish **five May 7–10 military deaths and one later death on 6 June 2025**. [The Army's contemporaneous June tribute](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/havildar-injured-in-operation-sindoor-succumbs-to-injuries/) confirms Havildar Sunil Kumar Singh died of Sindoor injuries. May's five and the final operation-associated six are different dated cumulative totals, **not conflicting body counts**.
- The one memorialised IAF death was [Sergeant Surendra Kumar of 39 Wing](https://nationalwarmemorial.gov.in/roll-of-honours/details/26644), described independently as a **medical assistant**, not a fighter pilot. His death is **not** proof of a jet shot down or its crew's fate.
- The [8 July 2026 Pahalgam warrant](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/j-k/jammu-court-issues-non-bailable-warrant-against-let-founder-hafiz-saeed/) against Hafiz Saeed belongs to **RC-02/2025/NIA/JMU**. A [September 2026 warrant](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/chandigarh-news/nia-court-issues-fresh-warrant-against-lashkar-chief-hafiz-saeed-101789197144507.html) concerns a **separate weapons case**. Neither is a criminal conviction; full signed court orders remain outstanding.

## Data standard

Every item is attributed by source ID and categorized by **type**, **review status**, **classification**, **confidence** and/or **evidence gap** as applicable. The structured registry currently contains a 79-event chronology, 127 assessed claims, nine initial target-site records, 46 contradictions, 63 open questions, eight dated/scoped casualty records and 138 source entries. Source URL repetitions are flagged and not counted as independent corroboration. It includes a deliberately marked repeated source reference; repeated references are **not** independent corroborations.

Do not equate:
- an official statement with independent confirmation of its substance,
- a chargesheet with a conviction,
- a satellite image of damage with proof of casualties or military-system destruction,
- a lost aircraft with general comparative superiority of the systems involved,
- an unresolved claim with a discredited claim.

Do not use present-day exploitable security vulnerabilities or private individual information in contributions.

## Editorial corrections

Every substantive change should cite original evidence, identify the prior statement, explain the correction and update the revision history in `research/checkpoint-03.md` or a later checkpoint. Uncertainty is published rather than filled in.

## Current limitations

This edition has **not** retrieved and independently reviewed complete NIA filed exhibits, confidential intelligence, original licensed imagery, comprehensive medical/casualty records, every military-briefing recording or the latest signed court orders. Comprehensive historical, treaty/legal, humanitarian and economic reconciliation remains incomplete.

Checkpoint 09 resolves India's five-versus-six military death reporting through six original dated memorial records, distinguishes a medical assistant from fighter aircrew and disambiguates separate Saeed warrant cases. Checkpoint 08 reconciles Pakistan's dated military casualty count (11 to 13), identifies two Bathinda crash-related civilian deaths and adds Poonch district recovery figures with deduplication warnings. Checkpoint 07 records the MHA evacuation and housing-relief baseline, May 2026 Uri reconstruction gaps, UN-filed targeting denials and tested versus operational civil-defence systems. Checkpoint 06 adds formally circulated Article 51 claims, UN-documented ceasefire positions, symmetric OHCHR communications and India's parliamentary answer. Checkpoint 05 clarifies UN/NIA source cutoff dates, a disavowed military identity dossier, a separate LeT case, original UN letter identifiers and the narrow Ratle order. Checkpoint 04 adds French parliamentary records, Rafale procurement inference checks, 2026 airfield imagery, an UN/NIA attacker-count discrepancy and the August treaty-award dispute. The earlier October 2026 revision distinguishes combat effects from claims, no-expiry cessation terms and disputed legal justifications. An earlier revision compares the 7 May opening objectives and 8 May escalation claims with the July parliamentary account. It also covers the disputed August 2026 Indus Waters Treaty arbitral award and distinguishes Air India's conditional aviation-loss forecast from audited realized costs.
